Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Tailoo is a pet health journal and care‑routine app. This Privacy Policy explains how Tailoo may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Tailoo may collect account information such as your name, email address, sign‑in provider, subscription status, and language preference. Tailoo may also collect pet profile information that you choose to enter, including pet name, species, breed, age, photos, health goals, daily logs, schedules, medication notes, food preferences, and lab report files.

Guest, Freemium, and Premium Data

Guest mode is designed for temporary use and does not preserve records after the session, app deletion, or device change. Freemium records are stored on the device. Premium records may be synced to cloud services so you can restore records across devices.

How We Use Information

Tailoo uses your information to provide pet care journaling, schedule reminders, health reference summaries, food analysis, export tools, account support, and subscription access. Tailoo does not sell your personal information.

Photos and Files

Pet photos and uploaded lab reports are used only to support the features you choose. When Tailoo uses cloud processing or third‑party services, that processing is limited to operating the app features described in this policy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Tailoo may use notification permissions to remind you about feeding, medication, grooming, vet visits, or other routines.

Third‑Party Services

Tailoo may use services such as Apple Sign In, App Store subscriptions, cloud database or storage providers, customer support tools, or email delivery providers. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the app features.

Tailoo does not currently use third‑party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before enabling it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Tailoo keeps records only as long as needed for the plan you use, legal obligations, safety, account support, or service operation.

You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app: open Profile → Data and Privacy → Delete account and data. This permanently removes your account and your pet, health, and record data from your device and from our cloud storage. If you are unable to use the in‑app option, you may also email Tailooapp@gmail.com from your registered email address and we will process the deletion.

Security

Tailoo uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No mobile app or internet service can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

Children

Tailoo is intended for users who can lawfully create an account in their region. If a minor uses Tailoo, a parent or guardian should review and consent to use.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Tailooapp@gmail.com.
