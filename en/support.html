Support

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Contact

For support, account questions, privacy requests, or account deletion, contact Tailooapp@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Deleting your account

The fastest way is in the app: go to Profile → Data and Privacy → Delete account and data. This immediately signs you out, clears local data, and deletes your cloud records and account.

If you need help, email Tailooapp@gmail.com from your registered email address. We may ask you to verify ownership before deleting cloud account data.

Medical note. Tailoo is a pet record‑keeping and care‑routine app. It does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment, emergency care, prescriptions, medication dosage decisions, or medical procedures.
