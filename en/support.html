Support

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Contact

For support, privacy requests, or questions about your data, contact Tailooapp@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Deleting your data

Tailoo does not need an account. In the app, go to Profile → Data and Privacy → Delete all data to permanently delete everything on this phone and in your iCloud backup.

New phone or reinstall

Premium backs up to your iCloud automatically: sign in to the same Apple ID on the new phone, open Tailoo, and your data comes back. On any plan you can export a backup file from Profile → Data and Backup and import it on the new phone.

Medical note. Tailoo is a pet record‑keeping and care‑routine app. It does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment, emergency care, prescriptions, medication dosage decisions, or medical procedures.
